# Andromeda and Creative Diversity: The Lake (45s, 1080x1920)

Analogy: Meta's ad system is a lake. Every person is a different fish. Your ad creative is the bait.

| Time | Voiceover | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 5s | "You made ten ads. Meta only saw one." | Ten nearly identical worm lures drop onto the screen, then snap together into one. |
| 5 to 14s | "Think of Meta's ad system, Andromeda, as a huge lake. Millions of people swimming in it, and every one is a different kind of fish." | Camera swoops down into a stylized 3D lake full of different coloured fish schools. Label "Andromeda" floats over the water. |
| 14 to 22s | "You used to pick where to fish. That was targeting. Now Andromeda picks the spot for you. The only thing you control is the bait." | A fisher in a small boat points at a map, the map flies away, and the boat glides to a spot on its own. Spotlight on the hook. |
| 22 to 31s | "Most brands drop in ten copies of the same worm in different colours. To Andromeda, that is one bait, and it only catches one kind of fish." | Ten worms in slightly different colours sink. Only the orange fish come near. The other fish swim past. |
| 31 to 40s | "Diverse creative is a full tackle box. A new hook, a new angle, a new face, a new format. Each one catches fish the others never would." | A tackle box opens, and four very different lures fly out (spinner, fly, shiny spoon, frog). Each school lights up and bites. |
| 40 to 45s | "Your creative is your targeting now. Adwave builds the tackle box." | Pull back to the full lake with lines everywhere. Adwave logo and call to action. |

Fact basis: Andromeda is the first stage of Meta's ad delivery. It narrows tens of millions of candidate ads to a few thousand for each person (Meta Engineering, Dec 2024). The video makes no claims about a similarity percentage threshold, because Meta has not confirmed one.
