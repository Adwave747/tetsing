# Andromeda 2.0 Explained: Info Explainer (90s, 1080x1920)

Audience: advertisers who already know Andromeda but missed the new update.
Topic: The next generation of Andromeda that Meta published on October 28, 2026, and how it connects to the Creative Diversity rating in Ads Manager.
Style: info explainer. Bold on-screen headlines for each section, with the 3D lake visuals underneath as the illustration.

## Fact basis

- On October 28, 2026, Meta Engineering published the next generation of Andromeda. It replaces a single user model ("user tower") with an ensemble of specialized expert towers. Those draw on recent ad engagement, user cohorts, LLM-derived understanding of content, and activity across Facebook and Instagram (Meta Engineering, PPC Land, Social Native).
- Meta reports the system can process over a billion ads per second. Sources quote both "over 1 billion per server" and "over 10 billion per Grace Hopper chip", so the script uses the lower figure.
- Meta reports a 2.4% increase in conversions on Facebook since 2025. This is Meta's own figure with no published baseline, so the video attributes it to Meta.
- The Creative Diversity rating appeared in Ads Manager on August 26, 2026, rating each ad set Low, Medium or High (agency reports, including Common Thread Co, Mintec and Marketing Concurrent). Meta labels it as estimated and in development.
- The video does not claim a fixed reach penalty, a similarity percentage or a required number of ads, because Meta has not published any of these.
