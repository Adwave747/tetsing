## Fact basis

- The Creative Diversity rating appeared in Ads Manager on August 26, 2026. It rates each ad set as Low, Medium or High and is found under Columns, Customize columns, Creative diversity (agency reports, including Common Thread Co, Mintec and Marketing Concurrent).
- Meta labels the rating as estimated and in development and has not published its thresholds. Agencies report that it groups visually similar images and video thumbnails, and that many varied accounts still score Low.
- Andromeda is the first stage of Meta's ad delivery. It narrows tens of millions of candidate ads to a few thousand for each person (Meta Engineering, Dec 2024).
- The video does not claim a fixed reach penalty or a similarity percentage, because Meta has not confirmed either.
