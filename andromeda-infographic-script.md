# Andromeda 2.0: Infographic Explainer (about 90s, 1080x1920)

Style: flat 2D motion infographic, with no analogy. Diagrams, numbers, timelines and charts animate in time with the voiceover. It uses a dark navy background with bright accent colours, the same palette as the lake video, so the two feel like a series. Captions run at the bottom and a source note sits under each stat.

## Fact basis

- Andromeda is the retrieval stage of Meta's ad delivery. It narrows tens of millions of candidate ads to a few thousand for each person, before later ranking stages (Meta Engineering, Dec 2024).
- On October 28, 2026, Meta Engineering published the next generation of Andromeda. It replaces a single user tower with an ensemble of specialized expert towers. Those draw on sequential ad engagement, user cohorts, LLM-derived semantic features and cross-surface co-engagement on Facebook and Instagram.
- Meta reports over a billion ads processed per second and 300x fewer inference-time FLOPs than the 2024 Andromeda model. The voiceover says "300 times less compute", which simplifies "fewer inference FLOPs". The billion figure uses the lower of the numbers quoted in coverage.
- Meta reports a 2.4% increase in Facebook conversions since 2025, with no published baseline, so the video attributes it to Meta.
- The Creative Diversity rating appeared in Ads Manager on August 26, 2026, rating each ad set Low, Medium or High (agency reports). Meta labels it as estimated and in development and has not published its thresholds. Agencies report that it groups visually similar images and thumbnails. The "new headline counts as one" line reflects that reporting.
